What This Looks Like In Practice

When the brief doesn't fit a standard program

Some mandates don't map cleanly onto a multi-day Innovation Tour, a single-function Field Trip, or a closed-door Study Mission. A board wants a hybrid of site visits and private briefings. A team needs five cities in five countries. A university partner wants a joint program combining academic sessions with company visits. Custom Itineraries exist for exactly this — we start from your goals rather than from a fixed format.

How It Works

From first call to on-the-ground delivery

01

Discovery Call

We start with a conversation about your industry, team, objectives, and constraints — dates, budget range, group size, and any specific companies, technologies, or themes you want to cover.

02

Draft Itinerary

We return with a proposed route, day-by-day structure, and indicative pricing — built from our existing relationships and access across the region, not a generic template.

03

Refinement

We revise the draft with you until the itinerary reflects exactly what your team needs — swapping cities, adjusting pacing, or adding specific site visits and briefings.

04

Confirmation & Logistics

Once confirmed, we handle all on-the-ground logistics: transport, interpretation, visa guidance, and confirming access with every host on the itinerary.

05

On-the-Ground Delivery

A Nanyang Studios host travels with or meets your group at each stop, so the itinerary runs smoothly and any last-minute adjustments are handled in real time.

06

Post-Trip Report

A written summary of the itinerary, key takeaways, and any contacts or leads gathered along the way — something your team can circulate internally after you're back.

Who It's For

Any team with a mandate that doesn't fit elsewhere

  • Multi-market mandates spanning more cities or countries than a standard tour covers.
  • Hybrid programs combining academic partners, conferences, or internal offsites with field access.
  • First-time visitors who aren't yet sure which program fits and want to talk it through first.

What's included

Everything above — discovery, drafting, refinement, logistics, on-the-ground delivery, and a post-trip report — assembled around whatever combination of cities, formats, and access your mandate requires.
