Program III

Executive Study Missions

Senior leadership programs pairing site visits with closed-door briefings from operators, investors, and policymakers — built for the people setting strategy, not just observing it.

Duration 2–4 days
Group size 4–10 people
Cities Usually 1
Best for C-suite & board members

What This Looks Like In Practice

Small rooms, direct access, honest conversation

Executive Study Missions are built for a small group of decision-makers who need a candid, current read on a market — not a curated showcase. The group stays deliberately small, the agenda stays confidential, and the conversations are structured to be off-the-record so operators, investors, and policy contacts speak freely.

Site visits still happen, but they're secondary to the briefings: private sessions with people who are building, funding, or regulating the sector your leadership team is trying to understand.

Sample Itinerary

Three days in one city — a mission built around one strategic question

Day 1

Arrival & Private Briefing

Arrival, followed by a private briefing with an industry or policy expert on the current state of the market, then a working dinner discussion with the group to align on priority questions for the mission.

Day 2

Site Visits & Investor Roundtable

Morning site visits chosen to ground the previous day's briefing in what's actually happening on operating floors, followed by an afternoon closed-door roundtable with regional investors and operators.

Day 3

Strategic Workshop & Departure

A facilitated workshop translating what the group heard and saw into implications for strategy, followed by a written executive debrief and departure.

This itinerary is illustrative. The specific briefings, contacts, and site visits are assembled around the strategic question your leadership team brings — a market-entry decision, a competitive threat, a regulatory shift — and confidentiality arrangements are set up before the mission begins.

Who It's For

The people who set strategy, not just report on it

  • Chairmen, CEOs, and board members forming a view ahead of a major strategic decision.
  • C-level executives — Chief Innovation, Data, or Strategy Officers — building the case for a new market or capability.
  • Investment committees conducting on-the-ground diligence before a regional commitment.

What's included

Senior-level access coordination, confidentiality and off-the-record arrangements with all briefing contacts, bespoke pre-mission briefing materials, private transport and interpretation, and a written executive debrief after the mission.

Format & Investment

Confidential by design, quoted individually

Because access and confidentiality arrangements are built specifically for each mission, pricing is scoped individually — starting with a discovery call where we discuss the strategic question driving the trip.
