Our Philosophy

Understanding a place means standing inside it

A slide deck can tell your team what Shenzhen's hardware ecosystem looks like. Only a factory floor can tell them what it feels like. We design journeys that swap the conference stage for the workshop bench, because insight travels further when it's earned firsthand — in the smell of solder, the pace of a livestream warehouse, the quiet of a government lab at 8am.

Field Notes

Dispatches from where we work

Dispatch No. 01

Singapore

A garden city built on order and ambition, where government-backed labs and fintech unicorns share the same tropical humidity. Start here to understand how a nation-state operationalizes innovation.

Dispatch No. 02

Shenzhen

Walk the electronics markets of Huaqiangbei and you'll understand why hardware ideas move from sketch to shelf in six weeks, not six months. This is where "good enough" gets replaced by "shipped."

Dispatch No. 03

Shanghai

Consumer China moves fastest here — new retail formats, livestream commerce, and global R&D outposts sit inside the same blocks as century-old shikumen lanes.

Dispatch No. 04

Hangzhou

Home to the platforms that rewired how a billion people pay, shop, and borrow. A masterclass in digital-first scale.

Dispatch No. 05

Beijing

Deep tech and policy sit closer together here than anywhere else in the region — a useful vantage point for where regulation and research are heading next.

Dispatch No. 06

Regional Add-Ons

Tokyo, Seoul, Jakarta, and Kuala Lumpur extend any itinerary for teams mapping innovation across the wider region.
